# Tirek interface

## Direction
An operational purchasing workspace for sustained daytime use on a laptop. The task and scope are already specified in docs/platform-spec.md; the user delegated implementation and asked to proceed directly.

White work surface, subtly green neutral canvas, deep forest navigation, restrained emerald actions. Strong table hierarchy, tabular numerals, generous page gutters, compact row spacing. Manrope with self-hosted Cyrillic fonts. Phosphor icons. No marketing hero, decorative photography or fabricated business results.

## Sources and adaptation
- https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable: Operate mode, typography, hierarchy, purposeful states, bounded visual inspection.
- https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill: inventory/dashboard search, keyboard accessibility, bulk selection, responsive tables. The search's marketing landing-page pattern was unsuitable and deliberately discarded.
- https://github.com/leonxlnx/taste-skill: coherent palette, self-hosted type, consistent icon family. Its current main skill explicitly targets landing pages rather than dashboards; its marketing layouts and motion defaults are not applied.

## Components
Semantic tables, native labeled inputs, Radix dialogs for focused editing/confirmation. Buttons have consistent shapes and loading states. Side navigation collapses at tablet widths. Tables scroll within their own region. Status always has text as well as color. Reduced motion disables nonessential transitions.

## Product truth
Persistent synthetic-data notice; unavailable ML/AI labeled explicitly. All calculated business values arrive from the backend. Summary money is marked incomplete when any relevant price is unknown. Approval is a scenario snapshot, never transmission of a supplier order.
